# Day 2 — Rainfall Data Pipeline

## Objective

Obtain real rainfall data for the North Eastern Region of India and prepare it for landslide risk prediction.

## Target States

1. Arunachal Pradesh
2. Assam
3. Manipur
4. Meghalaya
5. Mizoram
6. Nagaland
7. Sikkim
8. Tripura

## Required Features

- rainfall_24h
- rainfall_3day
- rainfall_7day

## Primary Source

India Meteorological Department (IMD)

## Future Use

Rainfall features will be combined with terrain, soil moisture, satellite and historical landslide data for ML-based landslide risk prediction.
